## Image generation prompt

Built-in installed image tool, one image; no CLI or external runtime dependency.

> Use case: historical-scene. Generate a single sparse prehistoric cave-pigment motif centered on plain light warm ochre sandstone: one large broken circular charcoal stroke enclosing a much smaller red-ochre circular loop, with one tiny chalk-white dab inside the small loop. This symbolizes a small measured amount within a greater whole. Motif occupies central 65 percent of square. Flat hand-painted worn earth pigment, uneven finger and burnt-stick lines; bare stone visible within both circles. No shading that makes objects three-dimensional. Rock texture very faint so dark pigment can be transferred onto an inherited cave wall. No text, letters, numbers, captions, glyphs, signatures, logos, borders, people, frogs or hands. Only the two nested circles and small chalk dab, not a diagram or polished icon.

The final image is an additive composite of this generated pigment and the
verified ancestor, not a regeneration of the ancestor's painting.
